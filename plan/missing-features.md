# Missing Features - Feature Gap Analysis

## Overview

This document lists 50 features that other platforms offer but ConnectZ currently lacks. This serves as a roadmap for future development prioritization.

Based on competitor analysis of Taplio, Expandi, PhantomBuster, Dux-Soup, Buffer, Hootsuite, and Sprout Social.

---

## Priority Features (High Impact)

### 1. AI Model Selection
- **What it is:** Allow users to choose between different AI models (GPT-4, Claude, Mistral, etc.)
- **Why needed:** Different models have different strengths - some are better at humor, others at formal content

### 2. Custom Prompts
- **What it is:** Users can save and reuse custom system prompts/templates for different use cases
- **Why needed:** Power users want full control over AI behavior and tone

### 3. Content Scheduling
- **What it is:** Schedule posts to publish at optimal times in the future
- **Why needed:** Users generate content but want to post when their audience is most active

### 4. Hashtag Suggestions
- **What it is:** AI generates relevant hashtags for each post based on content
- **Why needed:** Hashtags improve discoverability but are time-consuming to research

### 5. Comment Templates
- **What it is:** Save and categorize comment templates for different scenarios
- **Why needed:** Users have repeatable comment patterns they want to reuse quickly

### 6. Engagement Analytics
- **What it is:** Track likes, comments, shares on generated content over time
- **Why needed:** Users want to know if AI content actually performs well

### 7. Multi-Platform Support
- **What it is:** Support for Twitter/X, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok
- **Why needed:** Users manage multiple platforms, want single tool

### 8. AI-Powered Replies
- **What it is:** Auto-generate replies to comments on user's own posts
- **Why needed:** Scale engagement without manual response to every comment

### 9. Content Recycling
- **What it is:** Repurpose old high-performing posts into new variations
- **Why needed:** Good content can be refreshed and reused multiple times

### 10. Viral Post Detection
- **What it is:** Identify trending posts in user's niche to engage with
- **Why needed:** Early engagement on viral posts amplifies reach

## User Experience Features

### 11. Dark Mode
- **What it is:** Dark theme option for the extension popup and UI
- **Why needed:** Many users work late; dark mode reduces eye strain

### 12. Keyboard Shortcuts
- **What it is:** Quick actions via keyboard (e.g., Ctrl+G to generate)
- **Why needed:** Power users prefer keyboard over mouse clicks

### 13. Export History
- **What it is:** Export all generated content as PDF or DOCX
- **Why needed:** Users want a backup or to share with managers

### 14. Content Library
- **What it is:** Save and organize generated content for later use
- **Why needed:** Generated content can be used across different posts

### 15. Bulk Generation
- **What it is:** Generate 10+ posts at once from a list of topics
- **Why needed:** Content creators need volume efficiently

### 16. Content Templates
- **What it is:** Pre-built templates for announcements, promotions, thought leadership
- **Why needed:** Faster content creation for common use cases

### 17. Character Counter
- **What it is:** Show character/word count with LinkedIn limits
- **Why needed:** Prevent posts being cut off by LinkedIn limits

### 18. Hashtag Generator
- **What it is:** Batch generate hashtags for any content
- **Why needed:** Separate tool for hashtag research

### 19. Image/Video Suggestions
- **What it is:** Suggest visual content ideas to pair with posts
- **Why needed:** Visual content gets more engagement

### 20. Trending Topics
- **What it is:** Display trending topics in user's industry
- **Why needed:** Content ideas based on what's working now
